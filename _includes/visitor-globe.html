{% comment %} Visitor globe at the foot of the rail: a self-hosted canvas globe (assets/js/visitor-globe.js, coastlines in assets/js/globe-land.js) with one crimson diamond per visitor city, sized by count. Counts come from the Cloudflare Worker in _cloudflare/visits/ via `site.visitor_stats_url` (POST /hit once per browsing session, GET /stats). With the URL empty the globe still renders and turns, just without marks or the "N visits · M places" line. {% endcomment %}
