## Teaching

I taught for five years at WPI as a graduate teaching assistant, from the first-year robotics sequence to graduate computer vision, and hold a Certification in College Teaching from the Higher Education Consortium of Central Massachusetts (2017–2019).

<div style="margin-left: 20px;" markdown="1">
**RBE 549 · Computer Vision** (graduate) — *Fall 2022, Fall 2018*  
Co-designed and delivered the Fall 2022 edition of the course, covering classical and deep-learning approaches, with weekly lectures and office hours supporting student projects ([course site](https://nitinjsanket.github.io/teaching/rbe549/fall2022.html)).

**RBE 3001 & 3002 · Unified Robotics III & IV** — *2020–2021*  
Supervised the hands-on labs — kinematics and control of a 3D-printed robot arm, mobile-robot navigation — and designed and graded the final projects.

**RBE 1001 · Introduction to Robotics** — *2017–2018*  
Managed and mentored a team of five undergraduate peer learning assistants; designed and graded projects, lab reports and homework.
</div>

## Mentoring

Outside the university I coach school robotics teams: certified REC Foundation coach (2023–2024) and certified FIRST Tech Challenge coach (2022–2023), guiding students through robot design, programming and competition strategy.

## Academic service

**Journal reviewer** — IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L); IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems; IET Cyber-Systems and Robotics; Discover Robotics; The Journal of Supercomputing.

**Conference reviewer** — ICRA, IROS, CDC, ACC, ECC, and the International Conference on Ubiquitous Robots (UR).

**Memberships** — IEEE, IEEE Young Professionals, IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, Association for Women in Mathematics, and the Alpha Chapter of Rho Beta Epsilon (robotics honor society) at WPI.

## Leadership

As **President of WPI's Graduate Student Government** (Jan. 2019 – May 2020) I led the organization's governance and served as the primary liaison between the graduate student body and the administration — escalating housing concerns to the Board of Trustees with the Graduate Studies Office, and serving on the Provost Search Committee. WPI's [profile of the Class of 2020](https://www.wpi.edu/news/gsg-president-fondly-recalls-graduate-class-2020) has a few words from that year.

Earlier, I volunteered with the Lhasa Welfare Center for Children (2013), fundraising for children's education and tutoring Chinese, math and English.
