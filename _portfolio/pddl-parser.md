A small, dependency-light Python parser for PDDL domains and problems: it reads types, predicates, actions with preconditions and effects, and the initial state and goal of a problem, producing plain Python structures that are easy to feed into planners or into the transition systems used in my game-theoretic and temporal-logic planning work.

Source and usage: [github.com/leelening/pddl-parser](https://github.com/leelening/pddl-parser).
