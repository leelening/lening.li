I taught for five years at WPI as a graduate teaching assistant, from the first-year robotics sequence to graduate computer vision, and hold a Certification in College Teaching from the Higher Education Consortium of Central Massachusetts (2017–2019).

## Courses

<div style="margin-left: 20px;" markdown="1">
**RBE 549 · Computer Vision** (graduate) — *Fall 2022, Fall 2018*  
Co-designed and delivered the Fall 2022 edition of the course, covering classical and deep-learning approaches, with weekly lectures and office hours supporting student projects ([course site](https://nitinjsanket.github.io/teaching/rbe549/fall2022.html)).

**RBE 3001 & 3002 · Unified Robotics III & IV** — *2020–2021*  
Supervised the hands-on labs — kinematics and control of a 3D-printed robot arm, mobile-robot navigation — and designed and graded the final projects.

**RBE 1001 · Introduction to Robotics** — *2017–2018*  
Managed and mentored a team of five undergraduate peer learning assistants; designed and graded projects, lab reports and homework.
</div>

## Mentoring

At Harvard I advise student researchers in Prof. Na Li's group on algorithm design, system evaluation and reproducible methodology. Outside the university I coach school robotics teams as a certified REC Foundation coach (2023–2024) and certified FIRST Tech Challenge coach (2022–2023), guiding students through robot design, programming and competition strategy. I have coached [Vinci STEAM](https://www.vincisteam.com)'s middle-school VEX team **10102Z** since the 2023–2024 season; in 2024–2025 the team won the **Middle School World Championship** at the [2025 VEX Robotics World Championship](https://www.vincisteam.com/post/global-stage-vinci-pride-vinci-steam-dominates-vex-worlds) in Dallas.
